About this demo

Speech is recognised by your browser (Chrome, Edge or Safari) — nothing is recorded or stored; phrases are spoken with the browser's British English voice. Payment is a demo — no card is charged. Statistics show their source and year on screen. Analytics events are logged to the browser console.
